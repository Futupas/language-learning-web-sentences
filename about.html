About / Legal

This is a language-agnostic flashcard application designed to make learning vocabulary seamless and intuitive. It runs entirely on the client-side, storing progress locally on your device.

Available Courses

Test out the different vocabulary sets by clicking the links below. The app dynamically loads the configuration from the URL.

  • 🇪🇸 Spanish Basics (words_es)

Open Source

Created with vanilla TypeScript, SCSS, and Vite. This project is entirely open-source. Feel free to view the code, contribute, or fork the repository to create your own courses!
